Uncertain dates

Family Tree Builder: how to write a date you're not sure of

Type dates the way you'd say them. How sure you are goes into the date itself: a date you're not sure of is drawn in pencil on the tree, and one you know is in ink.

When sources disagree

When two records give different dates, write both with "or". It's drawn in pencil and noted as "sources disagree" until you settle it.

You typeShown as
1800 or 18011800 or 1801
19 or 30 Aug 196019 or 30 Aug 1960
Aug or Sep 1960Aug 1960 or Sep 1960

Behind the scenes

Dates are kept in EDTF, the Extended Date/Time Format (part of ISO 8601), a standard way of writing uncertain dates: 1858~ about, 1858? not sure, 1858% both, 188X a decade, ../1901 before, 1901/1911 a span, [1800,1801] one of these. You can type these too. The full standard is at the Library of Congress.
