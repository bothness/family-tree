Privacy

Family Tree Builder, the family's private tree at ourfamilytree.pages.dev

This site is a private family tree. Only people the tree's owner has invited can sign in and see it.

Signing in

You sign in with Google. Google tells this site only your name and email address, which are used to check that you've been invited and to show who made a change. The site never sees your Google password and asks Google for nothing else.

What's kept, and where

  • The family tree you and others add (names, dates, places, notes, photos), with a history of saved versions.
  • The list of people invited: their email address, name and role (owner, editor or viewer).
  • A sign-in record for each device you're signed in on, for up to 30 days. Only a scrambled form of its key is stored.

These are stored on Cloudflare (D1 and R2), in the tree owner's account. A copy of the tree is also kept in your own browser, so it works offline; signing out removes it from that device.

Cookies

One cookie, to keep you signed in. No advertising, analytics or tracking cookies.

Other services the site uses

  • Google, to sign you in.
  • OpenStreetMap (Nominatim), only when you search for a place: the place name you type is sent to it.
  • OpenFreeMap, for the map's tiles, and Google Fonts, for the lettering. These see your device's address, as any website you load from does.

Who can see the tree

Everyone the owner has invited. The owner can remove people at any time, which signs them out straight away. Nothing is shared with anyone else, sold, or used for advertising.

Removing your details

Ask the tree's owner to remove you from the invite list, or to remove or change anything about you in the tree.
